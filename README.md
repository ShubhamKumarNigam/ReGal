# ReGal: A Framework for Instruction-Tuned and Reinforcement Learning based Legal Judgment Prediction in India

This paper presents an exploration of AI-driven methodologies for judicial decision-making in India, introducing **Reinforcement Learning-based Legal Reasoning (ReGal)**. ReGal combines Multi-Task Instruction Tuning with Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback (RLAIF) to address the complexities of legal reasoning. While the framework is designed to refine its predictions through reinforcement learning, utilizing expert-annotated judgments and explanations as feedback, our results show that ReGal faces significant challenges. These include the task complexity, document length, and limitations arising from the use of pre-trained language models not specifically adapted to Indian legal corpora. Although ReGal underperforms compared to other models, this work provides valuable insights into the potential of reinforcement learning for legal tasks and highlights key areas for future research and optimization. Our study represents an initial step toward integrating AI into the Indian legal system, offering important lessons for further advancements in AI-driven legal frameworks.
